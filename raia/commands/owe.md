argument-hint: "[as at YYYY-MM-DD]"

Call the `payables` tool from the `raia` server. If a date was given here
("$ARGUMENTS"), pass it as `as_at`; if nothing was given, leave `as_at` out.

Answer with its opening figure first, as it is written, then the suppliers it
names. If it says the supplier list is out against the balance sheet, say that
before anything else. Do not add up or net any figures yourself.
